For a user-invoked requirement, `Verified` requires a production UI route through
the Windows adapter, C ABI, Core, and visible result; a smoke-only ABI call does
not substitute for that route. Internal build, ABI, renderer, resilience, and
portability requirements may be verified at their natural non-GUI boundary.
Representative current platform verification is summarized in
[`implementation-status.md`](implementation-status.md), not duplicated here.
